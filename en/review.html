3. Genres and design purposes

Simulation and action

Truck, flight, and space simulations use eye and head movement to adjust the view. Action games use gaze for aiming and target selection. Each entry records its documented platform and hardware. Euro Truck Simulator 2 · DCS World · Elite Dangerous · Deus Ex: Mankind Divided

Puzzles, narrative, and experimental mechanics

Stonesight lets players gaze at and stack stones. EyeChess uses gaze for chess selection. Before Your Eyes advances a memory narrative through blinking. SuperVision and Twileyed build challenges around peripheral vision and gaze aversion. Stonesight · EyeChess — endgame tutor · Before Your Eyes · SuperVision

Accessibility and training

EyeMine’s adapted keyboard, Eye Can Fly’s flight settings, and GazePlay’s mini-game suite provide different forms of gaze access. Attention tasks include rapid gaze shifts in Space Race, sustained fixation in Shroom Digger, target selection in Dr. Mole & Mr. Hide, and platform guidance in Kung Fall. These projects share categories and filters with the rest of the collection. Minecraft + EyeMine 2 · GazePlay · Space Race · Shroom Digger · Kung Fall

Research environments and evaluation

Atari-HEAD pairs gameplay actions with gaze. The Overcooked research environment studies collaboration. TrackMaze compares gaze, head movement, and tilt. InViRS and LEGO-puzzle studies examine social attention. Details record study design, sample, and publication type. Atari-HEAD — Atari research game suite · Overcooked — collaborative gaze research environment · TrackMaze · InViRS — Tangram & Bubble Popping · LEGO Puzzle — joint-attention study

VR

Synapse selects telekinesis targets with gaze and manipulates objects with controllers. Rez Infinite provides gaze aiming in its PS VR2 edition, while Switchback links blinking to enemy behavior. Synapse · Rez Infinite · The Dark Pictures: Switchback VR

5. Cognitive and perceptual training

The related collection covers Recollect, Recall, Remember Bee, Poly Rules!, Saving Memo, Sightseeing, and Listen from the Brain Game Center, organized by memory, cognitive flexibility, vision, and hearing. A 2025 gaze-contingent framework documents eye tracking that updates a simulated central scotoma in real time. The gaze-contingent framework is included under Research prototypes, with gaze-responsive interaction and perceptual-learning tasks.
